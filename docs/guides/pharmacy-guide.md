# Jansetu Workspace: Pharmacy Guide

**What you can do here:** book doctor consultations for walk-in patients and pay from your Jansetu wallet or by card or UPI, handle medicine orders, check prescriptions, add and import stock, look after your stock batches and suppliers, see what is running low, deal with held or recalled stock, and set your pharmacist.

Website: **https://staff.swasthadisha.etryx.in**

---

## 1. Sign in

1. Open the website.
2. Under **I am signing in as**, choose **Pharmacy staff**.
3. In **Mobile number or email**, type your pharmacy's registered mobile number (or its email, if the Jansetu team has added one).
4. Tap **Send code**. A 6-digit code arrives by SMS or email.
5. Type it in **6-digit code** and tap **Verify and sign in**.

No code? Wait for the timer, then tap **Resend code**. Typed the wrong number? Tap **Change number or email**.

The menu is on the left, under **Pharmacy**: **Book consultation**, **Booked consultations**, **Wallet**, **Orders**, **Prescription review**, **Stock**, **Suppliers**, **Low stock**, **Quality** and **Settings**. To leave, tap **Sign out** at the bottom of the menu.

---

## 2. Before your first order: set your pharmacist

Every prescription decision records your pharmacist's name. Set it once:

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Fill in **Pharmacist name** and **Registration number**.
3. Tap **Save**. You will see "Saved."

## 3. Orders

Open **Orders** to see all your orders, newest first. Use the **Status** box to show only one kind (for example **Placed**). Tap an order number to open it.

An order marked **Buying** is one your pharmacy bought from another pharmacy. You can only read it; the selling pharmacy manages it.

### Order statuses

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Placed | New order. Accept it. |
| Accepted | You have taken the order. |
| Packed | Medicines are packed. |
| Ready for pickup | Waiting for the customer to collect. |
| Out for delivery | On its way to the customer. |
| Delivery failed | The delivery did not work. Choose what happens next. |
| Cancellation requested | Someone asked to cancel a paid order. It waits for a decision. |
| Completed | Done. Stock is taken out of inventory. |
| Cancelled / Rejected / Abandoned | Closed. Nothing more to do. |

Payment shows as **Unpaid**, **Authorised**, **Paid**, **Partly paid**, **Payment failed** or **Refunded**.

### Move an order forward

1. Open the order. Under **Next step** you see the buttons you can use now.
2. Tap the next button, in this order: **Accept order** → **Mark packed** → **Ready for pickup** (or **Out for delivery**) → **Mark completed**.
3. Each button asks you to confirm. You can add a **Note** if you like. The customer is told about each move.

If a button is greyed out, the reason is written next to it:

- "Waiting for payment." The customer has not paid online yet.
- "The prescription must be approved first." Review it first (see section 4).
- "Record the payment first." You cannot complete an unpaid order.
- "A refill order is confirmed only after the customer pays."

### Record a cash payment

For orders paid at the counter or on delivery:

1. Open the order and tap **Record payment**.
2. Check the amount and tap **Yes, payment received**.

### Delivery problems

When an order is out for delivery (or ready for pickup), a **Delivery problem** area appears:

- **Delivery failed**: the delivery did not work. You can then retry, switch to pickup or record a refusal.
- **Retry delivery**: send it out again.
- **Switch to pickup**: the customer collects it at the pharmacy instead.
- **Refused at the door** or **Not collected**: the order is cancelled, the stock is released and, if it was paid, the customer is refunded.

Past problems are listed under **Delivery history**.

### Cancel an order

You can cancel while the order is **Placed**, **Accepted** or **Packed**:

1. Open the order and tap **Cancel order**.
2. Write a reason and confirm.

- **Unpaid order:** it is cancelled at once and the stock goes back.
- **Paid order:** it becomes **Cancellation requested** and cannot move until someone decides. A **Cancellation requested** box appears on the order with two buttons:
  - **Approve cancellation**: the order is cancelled, stock goes back and the customer is refunded.
  - **Keep the order**: write why (the customer sees it). The order carries on from where it was.

Customers can also ask to cancel paid orders. You decide them the same way. A Jansetu admin can decide them too.

From **Ready for pickup** or **Out for delivery** there is no **Cancel order**. Use a delivery problem option instead. If an open order's payment shows as **Refunded**, it cannot be cancelled here; the order says so. Ask the Jansetu team to close it.

### Customer returns

On a **Completed** order you will see **Customer return**:

1. First check **Already recorded**, so you do not record the same return twice.
2. Tap **Record a return…**.
3. Enter the returned quantity per batch. For each, choose **Back to stock** or **Discard**.
4. Write a **Reason**. If you want to refund the customer, enter an amount in **Refund to the customer's wallet (₹, optional)**.
5. Tap **Record return**.

### Refunds on an order

If an order has a refund, a **Refund** box shows where it is. If it says "Waiting to credit the wallet", you can tap **Retry refund**.

## 4. Prescription review

**Prescription review** lists orders whose prescription your pharmacist must check before packing. The longest-waiting order is at the top.

1. Tap an order number.
2. Under **Prescription documents**, tap **Document 1**, **Document 2** and so on to open each one.
3. Read it, then tick **I have read document 1 and it is legible**. Do this for every document.
4. Tap **Approve**. Or tap **Reject…**, fill in **Reason for rejection** (the customer sees it) and tap **Confirm rejection**.

- **Approve** only works after every document is ticked.
- If the customer attached nothing, the screen says so. Check the prescription another way (for example on delivery, or by phone) before approving.
- Rejecting a paid order refunds the customer and releases the stock.
- If a file "could not be displayed", ask the customer for a clearer copy.

---

## 5. Stock

**Stock** shows your medicines and their batches.

1. Type a name, brand or generic name in **Medicine** and tap **Search**.
2. Each medicine shows its batches: **Batch**, **Expiry**, **Unreserved**, **Reserved** and **Status**.

Batch statuses: **On sale**, **Sold out**, **Expired**, **Quarantined**, **Recalled**.

What you can do with a batch (only the buttons that fit its status appear):

- **Quarantine**: stop selling the batch for now. Give a reason.
- **Release…**: put a quarantined batch back on sale. You can write off damaged units.
- **Return to supplier…**: send unreserved units back. Choose the **Supplier**, **Units** and **Reason**, then tap **Record supplier return**. Every active supplier is listed; with many, type part of the name in **Find the supplier**. If you have no supplier yet, tap **Add a supplier** first (see section 6).
- **Recall…**: stop the batch for good. This cannot be undone. You then see which open orders hold it and which customers received it.
- **Recall trace**: see that list again for a recalled batch.

### Add stock (one batch)

1. Open **Stock** and tap **Add stock**. Or tap **Add a batch** next to a medicine you already stock.
2. Under **Find the medicine**, type its name and tap **Search**. Tap **Choose** next to the right one.
3. Fill in **Batch number**, **Expiry date** and **Units received**. MRP, selling price, purchase price, rack and reorder level are optional. You can add a **Note**, for example the supplier's bill number.
4. Choose the **Supplier (optional)** who delivered these units. Only your active suppliers are listed (with many, type part of the name in **Find the supplier**). The supplier is recorded with this delivery, not with the batch: the same batch can be topped up later from another supplier.
5. Tap **Check and add**. Read the summary, then tap **Yes, add to stock**.

The units are on sale once added if the medicine has a selling price; a medicine without one is not on sale until you price it. The workspace cannot take the units out again, so check the number first. Expired stock cannot be added.

If the supplier was archived meanwhile, the screen says so and lists your suppliers again: choose another, or none.

The message after adding names the supplier only once the stock history shows it was recorded. "(supplier not recorded)" means the units were added without a supplier.

**Stock history**, at the bottom of the page, lists the latest stock changes: when, the medicine, how many units, the batch, the **Supplier** of each delivery, and how (by hand, import or order).

If the page opens with "Medicine not found — pick another", the link named a medicine that is not in the list: find the medicine and tap **Choose**.

If you see "This stock entry was not confirmed", do not type it in again. Tap **Send the same entry again**: the units are never added twice. Or tap **Check whether it was recorded** first. To stop it instead, tap **Give this up**: if it was not added, it can never be added now and you can add the units again; if it was added, the screen says so.

A medicine that is not in the medicine list yet cannot be added here. Add it with a file (below): a new name adds it to the list.

### Import stock from a file

1. Open **Stock** and tap **Import stock from a file**.
2. Make the file. Tap **Download the template (with an example row)**: it has every column name and one example row (delete the example row before importing; it is refused if left in). Or use your own Excel or CSV file (up to 10 MB and 200,000 cells; split a bigger file into several). The first row must have the column names listed on the screen, in capitals or small letters. One row per batch.

   **Dates are read day first.** Write the expiry date as **DD-MM-YYYY**, for example **31-12-2027**. 05-06-2027 is 5 June 2027. These also work:
   - 31/12/2027 (day first);
   - 2027-12-31 or 20271231 (year first);
   - 12/2027 or 12-2027, as printed on packs (the last day of that month);
   - a date cell in Excel.

   A row with any other date shows a problem and is not added. If a file writes a date month first (12/31/2027), that row is refused, and so is every date like 05/06/2027 in the same file (it could be read two ways): the import page says so. Write the dates day first and upload the file again.

   **Prices.** A price cell left **blank keeps the medicine's current price**. A price of **0 sets it to ₹0**: the check shows every such row with a warning. A new medicine without a selling price is not on sale until you price it.

   **New medicines.** A name not in the medicine list adds a new medicine. It **needs a prescription** unless its row says "no" under **prescription required**. Existing medicines are not changed by an import.

   Numbers may have commas between thousands, such as 1,000 or 10,00,000. Write paise with a point, such as 12.50. A price written as 1,5 or 12 rs shows a problem and is not added.
3. Choose **Which rows to add**: every row, only medicines already in your stock, or only new ones.
4. If the whole delivery came from one supplier, choose it under **Supplier of this delivery (optional)**. Or name a supplier on each row in the **supplier** column (as on your supplier list). It is recorded with every row that adds units.
5. Choose the file under **Stock file** and tap **Check the file**. Nothing is added yet. A large file can take up to two minutes.
6. The import page lists every row. Rows with a problem come first and say what is wrong. Each row shows the prices it will set: the number (₹0.00 for a 0), "Kept: ₹…" for a blank cell when there is a current price, and "No price yet" for a blank cell of a medicine you do not stock yet ("Not on sale until priced" for its selling price), and "No MRP yet" for a blank MRP of a batch you do not hold yet (MRP belongs to each batch). Wholesale and purchase prices are shown when a row changes them. For a new medicine it also says whether it needs a prescription. If the template's example row is still in the file, nothing can be added: delete that row and upload the file again. If you chose a supplier for the file but the server did not keep it, the page says so. Tap **Download the rows with problems** to fix them in a new file.
7. Tap **Add … ready rows to stock…**, read what will happen, and tap **Yes, add them**. The confirmation counts only the rows your choice in step 3 adds, names every price change, and says which new medicines are not on sale until priced. If the same file name was added in the last 24 hours, the page warns you first: adding it again adds the units again.

Not adding a checked file? Tap **Discard this preview…** (not possible while the rows are being added). Nothing from it is added. It is hidden on this device only (the server keeps it as checked, and it is added only if someone confirms it).

**Recent imports** on the import page lists every file you uploaded and what happened to it. If some rows failed while being added, open the import and tap **Try the failed rows again…**. If an import says **Adding stopped** (it has not finished for more than 15 minutes), tap **Try again…**. A row is never added twice.

If the answer to adding the rows does not arrive, the import page checks again on its own every few seconds and shows the result when it is there.

If the answer to **Check the file** does not arrive, tap **Check whether it was recorded**. If an import of the same file name started just then, the screen asks "Is this it?": tap **Open it**, or **Upload again**.

## 6. Suppliers

**Suppliers** lists the distributors you buy from. Returns to a supplier go to one of these.

- **Add a supplier**: fill in **Supplier name** (the rest is optional) and tap **Add supplier**. If it says "not confirmed", tap **Send the same change again**: the supplier is never added twice. Or tap **Give this up**: then it can never be added (if it already was, the screen says so).
- **Edit**: change the details and tap **Save changes**. If someone else changed the same supplier meanwhile, the screen shows their change; check it and save again.
- **Archive**: the supplier is no longer offered for returns or deliveries. Returns and deliveries already recorded keep it. An archived supplier cannot be brought back; add it again if you need it.

Use **Show** to see **Active**, **Archived** or **All** suppliers.

---

## 7. Low stock

**Low stock** lists medicines at or below their reorder level. Expired and held batches do not count. The **Refills due in 14 days** column helps you plan for regular customers.

---

## 8. Quality

**Quality** brings together stock problems:

- **Orders holding held stock**: open orders that hold quarantined or recalled units. Tap **Swap to good stock** to swap in units from good batches (the order keeps its status). If you cannot swap, tap **Cancel order**: it is cancelled and a paid customer is refunded.
- **Batches on hold**: every quarantined, recalled or expired batch.
- **Recent returns to suppliers**: what you sent back.

## 9. Book a consultation for a walk-in patient

Many patients have no app or phone of their own. You can book a doctor for them at your counter. You pay Jansetu the fee from your **Wallet**, or by card or UPI. You collect the same amount from the patient.

Booking for patients may not be switched on for your pharmacy yet. Then the page says "Booking for patients is not switched on yet."

1. Open **Book consultation**.
2. Type the **Patient’s mobile number** and tap **Find**. One number can be shared by a whole family.
   - If the number is on Jansetu, you see part of the account holder’s name (for example "Rina K."). This protects the patient.
   - If the number is new, type it again in **Type the number again**.
3. Under **Who is the patient?**, choose:
   - **The phone’s owner**, and type the patient’s full name; or
   - a family member you booked for before (shown by name); or
   - **A family member**: type their name and choose the **Relation**. Gender and date of birth are optional.
4. Read the sentence under the tick to the patient: you book for them, an account is made for a new number, their name, number and problem go to Jansetu and the doctor, and you can see the prescription (not the diagnosis) for 90 days. If they agree, tick **Patient agreed to this booking**. You cannot book without it. There is no code to type.
5. Tap **Next: choose the doctor**. Doctors near your pharmacy are shown first (**Near you**, then **Same city**). Use **Speciality**, **How** and **From day** to narrow the list.
6. Tap **Choose** next to a doctor, then tap a free time.
7. Choose how the consultation happens: **Video call**, **Phone call** or **At the clinic**. For video or phone, choose where the patient joins: **on this pharmacy’s device** or **on the patient’s own phone**.
8. Choose the **Language the patient speaks**. A coupon and a few words about the problem are optional. With a coupon, the price shown first is before the coupon; the coupon is checked when you pay, and you then see the price with it.
9. Tap **Next: check the price**. Check the details, the price and your wallet balance with the patient.
10. Under **How do you pay?**, **Pay from wallet** is chosen. Tap **Pay … from wallet**. You see **Booked** with a **booking reference**. Collect the amount from the patient.

**Pay by card or UPI instead**
1. At step 10, choose **Pay by card or UPI** and tap **Pay … by card or UPI**. The time is held for the patient, and the Razorpay page opens.
2. Pay on the Razorpay page. You see **Booked** and "The consultation is booked and paid by card or UPI." Collect the amount from the patient.
3. If you close the Razorpay page without paying, you see **Booking held: waiting for payment** and "Pay before HH:MM or the time is released." Tap **Pay now** to open the same payment again. If you did pay, wait a minute and tap **Check payment**.
4. If it is not paid in time, the time is released and nothing is booked. Book again if the patient still wants it.
- The patient and the doctor are told only once it is paid.
- "Card payment is not switched on yet": pay from the wallet for now.
- A free consultation (nothing to pay) is always booked with the wallet; nothing is taken from it.
- "The payment came after the booking could no longer take it": the money goes back to the card or UPI it was paid with. Nothing is booked.
- "The booking is held, but the card payment could not be started": tap **Send the same request again**. It is never booked twice.

**How the patient joins**
- Video on your device: open **Booked consultations**, open the booking, and tap **Join call**. It appears 10 minutes before the time.
- Video on the patient’s phone: the patient signs in to the Jansetu app with the booked number and taps Join.
- Phone call: the doctor calls your pharmacy, or the patient’s number.
- At the clinic: the patient goes to the doctor’s clinic at the booked time.

**If something goes wrong**
- "Your Jansetu wallet does not have enough money": tap **Top up now**, add money, then pay again.
- "The price has changed": tell the patient the new price, then tap **Use the new price**.
- "This time was just taken": choose another time.
- "Your Jansetu wallet is on hold" or "owes money": contact Jansetu support.
- "You have looked up many patients this hour": wait, then try again.
- "The outcome of this request is not known yet": tap **Send the same request again**. The patient is never booked or charged twice.

### Booked consultations

**Booked consultations** lists everything you booked, newest first. Use **Status** to show one kind. Tap **Open** to see one booking.

- **Waiting for payment**: a booking paid by card or UPI that is not paid yet. Tap **Pay now** in the list, then **Pay now** on the booking. It shows when to pay by; after that the time is released. **Pay now** is not offered in the last 2 minutes, and not again once Razorpay said the payment went through ("Razorpay reported the payment; it is being confirmed. Do not pay again"): then tap **Check payment**. On an older Jansetu server the payment page opens only in the browser tab where you booked it; elsewhere tap **Check payment**.
- "Payment was not completed in time": the card booking was not paid before its time, so it was released. Nothing was taken (a payment that arrives later goes back to the card).
- "Too many bookings are waiting for payment": pay or cancel one of them first.
- **Cancel**: tap **Cancel this consultation**, then **Yes, cancel it**. You can cancel any time before it starts. The full amount goes back the way it was paid: to your wallet, or to the card or UPI. Give the patient their money back.
  - For a booking paid by card or UPI, choose **Refund to card (default)** (it usually reaches the card in 5 to 7 working days) or **Refund to wallet** (at once; this money pays for bookings but cannot be withdrawn). After cancelling you see where the refund is: in progress, refunded, did not go through, or held for a check by Jansetu (Jansetu then contacts you).
  - A card booking cancelled before it was paid refunds nothing.
- **Cancelled by patient**: the patient cancelled it. The money is back in your wallet.
- **Prescription**: after the consultation you see the medicines, advice and tests. The diagnosis is never shown to pharmacies. Tap **Open the prescription copy** to see or print it.
- **Make a medicine order from this prescription**: for each medicine, tap **Search**, **Choose** the one you will give and type the **Units**. Leave out any you do not have. Tap **Check the order**, then **Make the order**. It appears in **Orders**, for pickup and payment at your counter.
- **Change time**: you can move the consultation to another free time of the **same doctor**, any time before it starts. Tap **Change time**, choose a **From day** and a new time, tap **Next: check the new time**, read it, then tap **Yes, move it**. The price stays the same: nothing more is taken from your wallet and nothing is refunded. The way it happens (video, phone call or at the clinic) stays the same, and so does the join link. The patient and the doctor are told the new time. Tell the patient too.
  - "This time was just taken": choose another time.
  - "This booking changed since you opened it": the page reads it again; choose the time again.
  - "Too late: the consultation has already started": it can no longer be moved.
  - "The outcome of this request is not known yet": tap **Send the same request again**. It is never moved twice. Or tap **Check whether it was recorded**.
- **Correct the name**: if you typed the patient's name wrong when booking, tap **Correct the name**, type it right and tap **Save the name**. This only changes the name **your pharmacy** sees for this patient. It never changes the patient's own Jansetu account. If the patient chose your pharmacy themselves (by ordering from it), you keep seeing the name on their own account.
  - For a booking for a **family member**, tap **Correct the name of the phone's owner** to correct the name of the account holder. The family member's own name is on the phone owner's account: only the phone's owner can change it, in their Jansetu app.
  - The button is not shown when your pharmacy has no link to this patient.

## 10. Wallet

**Wallet** shows the money in your Jansetu wallet. Bookings for patients are paid from it (or by card or UPI). Refunds of cancelled wallet bookings come back to it, and refunds of card bookings when you choose **Refund to wallet**.

- **Add money**: type the **Amount to add (₹)** and tap **Top up**. Pay by card or UPI on the Razorpay page. When it is done you see "… was added to your wallet."
- If you closed the payment page or lost the connection, the top-up shows under **Top-ups not confirmed yet**. Tap **Check my top-up**. If you paid, the money is added once. If it says "Not paid yet" and you did not pay, tap **Remove from this list**. If the payment page did not open at all, no money was taken: just try again.
- **Wallet history** shows every top-up, booking payment and refund. Money in shows with **+**, money out with **−**.
- **On hold**: the wallet cannot pay for bookings. Money can still be added. Contact Jansetu support.
- **Owed to Jansetu**: a card payment was disputed. The next money you add clears this first. Contact Jansetu support.

## Good to know

- Only the Jansetu team can create a pharmacy account or change your registered mobile number or email.
- Email sign-in works only after the Jansetu team adds your email to your account.
- You cannot pack or send out a prescription order until the prescription is approved.
- Completing an order takes its stock out of inventory. It cannot be undone.
- A paid order cannot be cancelled straight away. It waits for **Approve cancellation** or **Keep the order**.
- If you see "The outcome of this request is not known yet", tap **Send the same request again**. It is never recorded twice. (For **Add stock**, check first instead: see section 5.)
- Lists refresh on their own about every minute.

## Common questions

**I typed my email but it says "No account uses this email".**
Sign in with your mobile number. To use email sign-in, ask the Jansetu team to add your email.

**It says "This mobile number isn't registered as a pharmacy."**
Your pharmacy is not set up yet, or you used a different number. Ask the Jansetu team to add your pharmacy.

**The Approve button is grey.**
Open and tick every document first. Also check that your pharmacist is saved in **Settings**.

**Why can't I mark an order packed?**
Read the message next to the button. It is usually waiting for payment or for prescription approval.

**The customer paid cash. How do I complete the order?**
Tap **Record payment** first, then **Mark completed**.

**I quarantined a batch by mistake.**
Find it in **Stock** and tap **Release…**.

**How do I add a new supplier or new stock?**
Suppliers: open **Suppliers** and tap **Add a supplier**. Stock: open **Stock** and tap **Add stock**, or **Import stock from a file** for many medicines at once.

**The patient has no phone. Can I still book?**
Yes. Use the number of a family member who came with them, choose **A family member**, and book the video call on your pharmacy’s device.

**I was signed out suddenly.**
Your session ended. You may have been idle a long time, or someone else signed in on this browser. Just sign in again.
