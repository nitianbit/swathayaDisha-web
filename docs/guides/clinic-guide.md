# Jansetu Workspace: Clinic Guide

**For:** clinic owners and clinic staff, including a doctor's own clinic. **Website:** https://staff.swasthadisha.etryx.in

This guide says where things are. The names in it (Demo Care Clinic, Neha Demo, Clinic Desk Demo, Dr Kavya Demo) are examples.

Each section ends with a **Switch** line for the Jansetu team. The clinic pages need no switch: "None" means the page always works.

---

## Sign in

1. Under **I am signing in as**, choose **Lab or clinic staff**.
2. Type your **Mobile number or email** and tap **Send code**. Email works only if your owner has added it for you.
3. Type the **6-digit code** and tap **Verify and sign in**.
4. Choose your clinic (for example "Demo Care Clinic · Clinic · Staff") and tap **Open workspace**.

A new clinic opens once Jansetu has verified it. Until then Home says it "is waiting for verification by Jansetu".

**A doctor with their own clinic** signs in here too, with the same mobile number, and chooses the clinic. The doctor tools (Consult now, Appointments, Schedule) are under the **Doctor** sign-in.

On a phone, **Home** and **Menu** are the two buttons at the top. Tap **Menu** for every other page; it closes when you open one.

## Your reception home

Home is your reception. The big buttons depend on what you are allowed to do:

| Button | What it does | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **+ New** | Book an appointment or make a bill for a patient | Booking appointments or making bills |
| **Find patient** | Find a patient by phone, or by name among today's appointments | as + New |
| **New bill** | A new bill | Making bills |
| **Today** | Everything of the day | Appointments or bills |

If you may do none of these, Home says **Ask the clinic owner to allow them**.

Under the buttons:

- **Doctors today**: each doctor with their next appointments. A grey **Busy** block (for example **12:00–12:30 Busy**) means the doctor is busy elsewhere; you never see where or with whom.
- **Collected today ₹…** with the ways money came in, **To collect ₹…**, and **Refunded today ₹…** when there was a refund.
- **Waiting for you**: to check in, waiting for online payment, walk-ins without a bill, unpaid bills.
- **Today's register**: the latest 10 entries. An appointment and its bill show as one line, for example **Meena Iyer: Dr Kavya · 10:15 · In person · ₹500.00 · Paid (cash)**. An appointment on another day says its date.
- **My to-dos** (see below).

**Switch:** none for Home, Today and to-dos.

## + New: a walk-in, step by step

1. Tap **+ New**. Type the patient's **mobile number** and tap **Find**. The page says whether the number is on Jansetu.
2. Tap **This person** (type the name) or **Someone else (family member)** (name and relation). Tap **Next**.
3. Choose **Appointment now (walk-in)**, **Appointment for later** or **Bill**.
4. With several branches, tap the branch (the one you used last is marked **Used last**). Tap the doctor (with one doctor, it is chosen for you).
5. **Appointment now** shows **Next free: 10:15**. **Appointment for later**: tap **Today**, **Tomorrow** or **Pick a date**, then a free time. Times when the doctor is busy elsewhere are not shown.
6. Tap **Book Dr Kavya at 10:15, MG Road**. The bill opens with the doctor's fee on it (or your own consultation item for that doctor, at the same price).
7. Tap **Issue bill for ₹…**, how they paid (**Cash**, **UPI**, **Card** or **Bank**), then **Record ₹… paid**, then **Done**.

**Example: a walk-in with Dr Kavya paid in cash, 9 taps:** + New → **Find** → **This person** → **Next** → **Appointment now (walk-in)** → **Book Dr Kavya at 10:15, MG Road** → **Issue bill for ₹500.00** → **Cash** → **Record ₹500.00 paid**. Add one tap to choose the branch when you have two, and one to choose the doctor when the branch has several.

From **Find patient**, today's appointments of that number are listed; **Bill this appointment** makes the bill for one not billed yet.

## Today

The whole day, newest first, 50 at a time. Pick another **Day**; **Search the day** finds an entry by name, phone (4 or more digits) or reference.

**Switch:** none.

## To-dos

At the bottom of Home. Type a to-do and tap **Add**; tick it when done. Everyone at your clinic sees the same list (a member limited to some branches sees those branches' to-dos).

**Switch:** none.

## Where is what

**Owners** see every page. **Staff** see the pages their permissions allow. The owner gives permissions on **Staff**.

| Menu (section) | What is there | Staff need |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Your reception: big buttons, doctors today, money, register, to-dos | everyone |
| Today | Every entry of a day, with search | View bookings, Manage bookings or a bills permission |
| Profile (Organisation) | Your clinic's details and contact | everyone |
| Staff (Organisation) | Add staff and give permissions | Manage staff |
| Branches (Organisation) | Addresses, opening hours | Manage branches |
| Activity log (Organisation) | The latest 100 changes | Manage staff, all branches |
| Services and prices (Billing) | Your services and their prices | Manage tests and services, or Create invoices (view only) |
| Bills (Billing) | Bills made at your counter | Create invoices, Cancel invoices, Record payments or View finance |
| Finance (Billing) | Money Jansetu collected for you and paid out | View finance, all branches |
| Front desk (Clinic) | Appointments by day; book patients with your doctors | View bookings or Manage bookings |
| Doctors (Clinic) | The doctors who consult at your clinic | Manage staff, View bookings, Manage bookings or Create invoices |

"All branches" means the owner, or staff given **All branches, including new ones**. A desk person usually needs **Manage bookings**, **Create invoices** and **Record payments**.

## Organisation: Profile, Staff, Branches, Activity log

- **Profile**: your contact details, and **Allow part payments of bills**. Only an owner can change them. Jansetu changes registration and tax details.
- **Staff**: **Add a staff member** with name, mobile number, role, permissions and branches. On a row: **Edit permissions**, **Change branch access**, or remove them. You can give only permissions you hold yourself.
- **Branches**: **Add a branch** with its address, **Consultations at the clinic**, opening hours and holidays.
- **Activity log**: who changed what, newest first.

**Switch:** none.

## Services and prices

Your services (consultations, procedures and so on) with their prices. These appear on bills.

- **Add an item**: name, code, price, GST rate. Then **Add item**.
- On a row: **Edit**, **Deactivate** or **Reactivate**. Price changes apply at once.

**Switch:** none.

## Bills

Bills made at your counter, newest first.

1. Tap **New bill**, choose the branch and the patient.
2. Add items **From the catalogue** (your services and prices), or **Add a line by hand**. Choose the doctor if asked.
3. Tap **Review the bill**, then **Issue bill**.

On a bill: **Record payment** (choose how it was paid; nothing is chosen for you), **Cancel bill** (choose how any money was returned), **Open PDF**. You can also make a bill from an appointment on **Front desk** (**Create bill**).

**Switch:** none.

## Finance

Money Jansetu collected online for your clinic, and what it paid out. Bills paid at your counter are not part of it.

- **Balance**, **Statement** (with **Download CSV**), **Payouts**.
- **Where payouts are sent**: **Add payout details**. Owners only.

**Switch:** none.

---

## Front desk

Appointments with your doctors by day (India time), from your desk and online. Desk appointments are in person and paid at the clinic.

- Choose the branch, day and doctor. **Previous day**, **Today**, **Next day**.
- **Free slots**: tap **Book**, fill in the patient, then **Book appointment**.
- On an appointment: **Mark arrived**, **Mark as seen**, **Mark did not come**, **Move to another time**, **Cancel appointment**, **Create bill**.

**Switch:** none.

## Doctors

The doctors who consult at your clinic. The Jansetu team links a doctor to your clinic after checking them; there is no invitation to send. A linked doctor can be booked at your front desk.

- Owners can **Remove from this clinic**.
- No doctors yet? Ask the Jansetu team to link your doctors.

**Switch:** none.
