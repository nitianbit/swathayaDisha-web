# Jansetu App: Customer Guide

**For:** patients and their families. **Where:** the Jansetu app, or https://swasthadisha.etryx.in

This guide says where things are. The names in it (Asha Demo, Ravi Demo, Dr Kavya Demo, Demo Pharmacy, Demo Diagnostics) are examples.

One feature works only after Jansetu switches it on. Its section ends with a **Switch** line for the Jansetu team. "None" means it always works.

---

## Sign in

1. Type your **Mobile number or email** and tap **Send code**. A new user starts with the mobile number.
2. Type the **6-digit code** and tap **Verify and sign in**.

To sign in with email later, add it on **Profile** (**Verify and add email**).

## Where is what

The tabs at the bottom are **Home**, **Bookings**, **Medicines**, **Wallet** and **Profile**.

| Where | What is there |
|---|---|
| Home | Consult now, Book a doctor, Lab tests, Order medicines, My bookings, Wallet, Bills, Family members |
| Bookings | Your doctor bookings, lab tests and Consult now requests |
| Medicines | Order medicines from a pharmacy |
| Wallet | Your balance and history |
| Profile | Your details, email, family members, records requests, sign out |

---

## Book a doctor

1. **Home** › **Book a doctor**. Search by doctor or speciality and open a doctor.
2. Under **Choose a time**, pick a day and time, and how to consult (at the clinic, video call, phone call or chat).
3. Choose **For** (yourself or a family member), say what it is about, add a coupon if you have one, and tick **Use my wallet** if you like.
4. Tap **Book and pay**.

**Switch:** none.

## Consult now

See a doctor who is online now.

1. **Home** › **Consult now**. Choose how and a speciality, then **See who is online**.
2. Pick a doctor, or **Any available doctor**. Tap **Pay and request a doctor**.
3. Wait for a doctor to accept. When one does, tap **Join the call**.

If nobody accepts you can **Offer again**, or **Cancel and refund to my wallet**.

**Switch:** none.

## Medicines

1. Open **Medicines**. Search by pharmacy, area or pincode, open a pharmacy and **Add** medicines.
2. Tap **View cart**. Choose **Collect at the pharmacy** or **Deliver to me**, and how you pay.
3. If a medicine needs a prescription, attach a photo or PDF. The pharmacist checks it before packing.
4. Tap **Place order and pay**.

Your orders are under **My orders**.

**Switch:** none.

## Lab tests and reports

1. **Home** › **Lab tests**. Search by pincode or city and choose a lab.
2. Choose **Home collection** or **Visit the lab**, the branch and the tests. Tap **Book and pay**.
3. **Bookings** › **Partner lab bookings**: a **Report** badge means your results are in. Open the booking and tap **Get report**.

If the lab proposes a new time, tap **Accept the new time**.

**Switch:** none.

## Bills

**Home** › **Bills**: bills from labs and clinics you visited. Open one to see the items and **Get bill PDF**. Unpaid bills are paid at the counter, not in the app.

**Switch:** none.

## Wallet

Your **Balance** and **History**. Refunds come here. Tick **Use my wallet** when you book to pay with it.

**Switch:** none.

## Family members

**Profile** › **Family members you book for** › **Add a family member**. They then appear under **For** when you book.

**Switch:** none.

## Records requests from doctors

A doctor treating you can ask to see your prescriptions from other doctors and your lab reports.

- A **Requests to see your records** card shows on **Home** while a request is waiting. Tap **Allow** or **Deny**.
- **Profile** › **Requests to see your records** shows waiting requests, **Doctors who can see your records**, and past requests.
- To take access away, tap **Stop sharing**.

**Switch:** `RECORDS_ACCESS_ENABLED`. Until then: "Sharing records with doctors is not switched on yet."

## Profile

Your details, your email, **Sign out** and **Sign out on all devices**.
